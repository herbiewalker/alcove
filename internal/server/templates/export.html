Before you start

  1. Export a fresh backup on your phone first. In Tryst, open Settings, Backup & restore, Export encrypted backup. Restoring replaces what is on the phone, so anything you added there after your last upload would be lost without it.
  2. Come back here, tick the box below and download the file.
  3. On the phone, open the file from your Downloads in Tryst (Settings, Backup & restore, Import backup), enter the password, and leave Replace my current data with the backup (recommended) switched on. Do not switch it off: merging keeps entries you deleted here and removes the partner links of the others.
